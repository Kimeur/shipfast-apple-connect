# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (21 October 2026)

First version, taken from the release scripts of Duo Peas and Fold Days and generalized.

- `shipfast` skill: signing, app settings, listing and products, screenshots and header, build and upload, submit.
- `/shipfast` command: read-only status of an app on the way to App Review.
- Price gate (`--confirm-prices`), product gate on submit, whole-hour release check, `--dry` everywhere.
- Tests against a fake App Store Connect.

Not yet done: a run of the generalized scripts against a real account, checks on a real iPhone Duo (for the Duo screenshot sizes).
